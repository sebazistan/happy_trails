# CUBEA — Wild Ideas & Open Questions

## A. Wild ideas (tell me which to try)

**Mechanics that use what's already built**
1. **Barbudos** *(built)*. Rebel cubes grow beards the longer they serve; a bearded veteran is a living service record.
2. **Mail call** *(built)*. Letters read from each soldier's *actual* family graph — a brother jailed, children hungry in the dead season, a proud mother.
3. **Tobacco readers (*lectores*).** In cigar factories a hired reader read newspapers and novels aloud to the rollers. A sympathetic lector is a one-person radio station for a whole workshop — and a target for the police.
4. **Compadrazgo as a game system.** Godparent ties are the strongest non-kin edge. Baptisms and weddings let you *choose* a compadre; accepting one creates a binding edge that propagates loyalty — and obligation.
5. **A radio dial as the news UI.** Tune between *Radio Rebelde*, CMQ, a baseball game and dance music; each station shows different headlines from the same event log — and a credibility meter for yours.
6. **Rumour ripples.** Make gossip visible: tiny speech icons hopping between cubes along the family graph so players *see* the shock travel.
7. **Graph detective.** Informer hunting by deduction: who is related to whom, who got new boots, who had a brother in the barracks. The inspector already has the data.
8. **Obituaries & the People's Ledger.** Every dead Cubean gets a generated one-paragraph life story; the end screen is a memorial wall of those whose deaths *you* caused or prevented.
9. **Thought line.** A single generated line per person ("worried about his brother in prison; hungry") — the inspector as a character study.
10. **Time-traveller sight.** Beyond foresight tokens: a once-per-run **rewind of the last 24 hours** (already used on death), and a "ghost of what will happen" overlay showing a likely ambush site.

**Fiction & world**
11. **The Zafra as the heartbeat.** Strike calendars, sugar prices, cane fires, *tiempo muerto* hunger — and a *cane-fire sabotage* option with a human cost.
12. **Festivals** — Virgen de la Caridad (8 Sept), Carnival in Santiago (July), Día de Reyes, Christmas Eve (*Nochebuena*) — as pressure/relief events and intelligence windows.
13. **Baseball.** Army base teams vs. village teams; a game can be a ceasefire, a recruiting tool, a political statement.
14. **Smuggling by sea.** Fishing boats ferrying arms from Mexico/Costa Rica past the coast guard: a small stealth-navigation minigame; weather matters.
15. **Foreign actors.** US press (Matthews), the ambassador, casino mob money in Havana, arms embargo as a clock; kidnapping of US servicemen (Raúl, 1958) as a dilemma.
16. **Ageing and orphans.** Children whose father you killed grow up in the two-year span; some turn 16 with a grudge.
17. **Justice scenes.** A village tribunal where you pick jurors from the community; legitimacy depends on who sits and whether the accused's kin are heard.
18. **Hurricane season** and drought as external shocks that make *everyone* hungry — and test whether your promises survive scarcity.

**Meta**
19. **Other eras with the same engine** (the *time traveller* conceit): 1895 (Mariana Grajales and the Maceo family *in person*), 1933 (the Revolt of the Sergeants), 1898, 1961 (Bay of Pigs & the literacy campaign). Each has a different "winning formula" and a different catch.
20. **The mirror game:** play the *regime* — buy loyalty, run informers, keep the army fed and the US happy, and watch your own family graph rot. Same engine, opposite dilemmas.
21. **Asymmetric multiplayer** (regime vs. rebels over the same graph).
22. **Revolutions platform:** factions, planks and rosters as data → Algeria, Vietnam, Russia, Philippines, Iran, South Africa — with community mods.
23. **Educational mode** with a timeline, sources, primary-document unlocks and teacher notes.
24. **The post-victory game:** January 1959 onward — governing the coalition you built; the promises come due. (The real history is the reason this is interesting.)

## B. Open questions for you

**Identity & tone**
1. *Who is the player, exactly?* I made the circle a time traveller *wearing Fidel's place* (Raúl is "your" brother, "Fidel Castro · El Viajero" is the nametag). Alternatives: the traveller **is** an observer with a different name; or Fidel is an NPC the traveller advises. Which?
2. *How much should the game "side" with the rebels?* Currently: celebratory but critical (concealment mechanic, tyrant path, epilogue notes that the real regime became authoritarian). Dial it up or down?
3. *Violence:* gritty-but-stylised (cubes shatter; no gore). Do you want fewer or more blood decals? **Sexual violence** is never shown; keep it as an abstract "misconduct" decision?
4. *Language:* English with Spanish flavour now. Add full Spanish localisation? Which Spanish register (Cuban idioms)?

**Scale & structure**
5. *Real-time-with-pause (now) vs. a tactical planning mode* (Door Kickers style: queue orders while paused)?
6. *Scenes + island map (now) vs. one big seamless open world?* The seamless version is much more expensive; scenes with a map is a legitimate Jagged-Alliance-style choice.
7. *How many people?* 3,000 now; the database could be 10–20k. More people ≠ more game unless the UI can show them. Your target?
8. *Time span:* Dec 1956 → Jan 1959 (≈ 25 months) with a hard cap at end-1960. Include the 1959+ governing phase?
9. *Should non-violent victory be viable?* Currently possible-but-hard (strike + legitimacy collapse); no combat needed after the prologue. Make it rarer/harder, or an explicit "pacifist path" achievement?

**Content**
10. *Granma boat:* make the **voyage a playable opening** (seasick, bailing, a man overboard)? Currently a card.
11. *Roster:* expand with verified birthplaces (Moncada veterans, Directorio, PSP, journalists)? Who do you most want?
12. *Regions:* Sierra + Oriente now. Add Las Villas/Escambray (Che's and the Directorio's front), Camagüey, Pinar del Río?
13. *The Mariana Grajales platoon:* playable earlier as a *policy* with its own scenarios (assigning missions, sending couriers to Havana)?
14. *Race & religion systems:* Santería/Catholic syncretism and Afro-Cuban mutual-aid societies (*cabildos*, *sociedades de color*) as factions or as structure inside factions?
15. *US & Soviets:* include an international layer (embargo, CIA, the Mafia in casinos, Moscow) or keep to the island?

**Tech & production**
16. *Art direction in UE:* keep literal cubes in 3D, or stylise towards "toy diorama" with soft shadows and tilt-shift?
17. *Team and timeline?* PC-only, solo or small team? That decides whether to build the Mass-Entity crowd or ship with fewer, richer actors.
18. *Release target:* Steam early-access? A narrative-heavy edition for schools?
19. *Research partners:* historians, Cuban consultants (Havana and Miami), sensitivity readers — who can you reach?

**Design details**
20. *Foresight tokens:* difficulty dial (1/3/5 per run), or a mechanic with story uses (rewind, reveal informer, preview ambush)?
21. *Permadeath of named characters:* keep (Che can die), or protect a few "plot-armour" characters in Normal mode?
22. *Camouflage:* now a *tint + detection range*. Want an explicit "cover" action and a visible detection cone for enemies?
23. *Obedience:* telegraphed refusals feel right? Add "obey anyway"?
24. *Is the Director too visible?* The Journal shows its variables. Hide in release or keep as a "transparency mode"?
