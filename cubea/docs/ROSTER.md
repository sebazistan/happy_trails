# CUBEA — Historical Roster

The prototype includes **38 authored people** (plus the player, who wears Fidel's place) and **~16 generated Granma expeditionaries** drawn from ordinary families across the island. Every authored person gets a generated family and friends as well, so *nobody is isolated*; real relatives are added where known (the Castro family, Celia Sánchez's sisters, Frank País's mother and brothers, Haydée Santamaría and Armando Hart as spouses, etc.).

**Birthplaces are linked to game places:** if a birthplace is playable (Havana, Santiago, Manzanillo…) the person's relatives live there; otherwise it exists as an *off-map place* (Birán, Rosario, Artemisa, Cárdenas, Encrucijada, Cruces, Yara, Media Luna, Banes, Galicia, exile) shown on the island map and in the inspector ("Born in …"). Their relatives there are in the database and can be affected by events.

## Notes on handling real people

* Portrayal is *character-driven, not hagiographic*: traits are chosen to enable gameplay (Che is hard on discipline and a doctor; Camilo is fearless and loved; Raúl is organised and cautious; Almeida is steady under fire; Celia is the trusted organiser) and each has a short human description.
* **Regime figures** (Batista, Tabernilla, Ventura, Chaviano, Cantillo) are shown as historical officials with roles; allegations of torture and corruption are reflected only through gameplay consequences, not scripted cruelty. Pedro Sarría is included deliberately as the soldier who protected prisoners in 1953 — *soldiers can be more than their uniform*.
* The Church figure (Pérez Serantes) is neither villain nor hero: he helped save prisoners in 1953 and distrusted radicalism.
* Real women (Celia, Vilma, Haydée, Melba, Teté, Isabel) have operational roles, not decorative ones; the platoon is a playable decision, not a cutscene.
* **Real persons should be reviewed by historians and by Cuban consultants before publication.** Names of fictional extras are random combinations of common surnames; any resemblance to real private individuals is accidental.
