# CUBEA — every tracked variable (people, rebels, actors, vehicles, the world)

*Generated from the running game's data model plus the systems that write to it. "0–100" scales: higher is more unless noted. Fields marked **NEW** were added for climate/states (`climate.js`). Where a field is a number that drives behaviour, the file that uses it is named.*

## A. Every person (`p`, in `C.W.people`) — ≈3,100 of them
**Identity & family:** `id` · `first` · `last` · `last2` · `tag` (display name) · `nick` · `sex` · `yob` (age is computed from the date) · `status` (alive/dead/jailed/exile/away) · `settlement` · `home` · `birth` · `eth` · `mother` · `father` · `spouse` · `kids[]` · `lineage` · `hh` (household) · `rel[]` (typed edges: parents, siblings, friends, compadres, rivals, coworkers…) · `look{}` (face, body, hair, beard, glasses, hat, skin tone…) · `hair` · `beard` · `hat`.
**Work & standing:** `occ` (occupation) · `cls` (0 poor … 4 rich) · `fac` (faction) · `literate` · `lit` (literacy progress) · `donor` · `note` · `role` · `roster` (historical figure id) · `rosterV` (verification level).
**Loyalty & politics:** `stance` (what they show) · `trueStance` (what they feel, −100…+100) · `conv` (conviction/strength of it) · `covert` · `informant` · `revealed` · `hidden` · `iss[]` (positions on the 8 planks) · `sal[]` (which issues matter) · `traits[]` (brave, timid, idealist, devout, hothead, generous, veteran, stoic, cautious, handy…) · `mem[]` (memories: event, sign, intensity, who, text, kin) · `memCount` · `lastEvent` · `fear` · `fearOf`.
**Needs (0–100):** `needs.food` · `needs.water` · `needs.energy` · `needs.safety` · `needs.dignity` · `needs.hope`.
**Psychology (old):** `stress` · `morale` · `anger` · `fearBase`.
**Health:** `hp` · `maxHp` · `health` (long-term 0–100) · `bleed` · `limp` · `armHurt` · `cracks[]` (light wounds that heal) · `chunks[]` (serious wounds that do not) · `ill` ({kind: fever/dysentery/heatstroke, days}) · `died` · `diedBy`.
**Body (NEW):** `body.heat` (heat strain) · `body.chill` (cold strain) · `body.wet` (soaked) · `body.feet` (foot health) · `body.sleepDebt` (hours awake beyond rest, 0–48) · `body.hygiene`.
**Mind (NEW):** `mind.happiness` · `mind.boredom` · `mind.grief` (rises when a friend dies, fades) · `mind.trauma` (accumulates under fire, fades slowly).
**Skills & experience:** `exp` (combat experience 0–100) · `service` (days served).
**Equipment (persons):** `wpn` · `ammo` · `items{}` · `stash` (long gun hidden in towns) · `inv{}` (food, water, ammo, med, grenades, molotovs, shells, rockets, hoe, axe, seed, bucket, feed, firewood, stakes, hammock, primer, snare, line, tools, patch, hose, belt, plug, battery, tire, pamphlets…) · `rat{f,w}` (ration level food/water: 1 full, ½ half, 0 none) · `fresh` / `freshT` (fresh meat units and when) · `hotFed` (last day fed hot).
**Bonds:** `bonds{personId → 0–100}` (friendship as strong as kin at 100).
**Ideology (creed.js → ideology.js):** `cr{patria, tierra, justicia, disciplina, fe, igualdad}` (−1…+1) · `zeal` (0–100) · `wantsJoin` (day they asked to enlist) · `routine[]` (scheduled actions) · `mealDone` (last meal slot).
**Military record:** `mil{side, rank, squad}` · `side` · `occDone`.

## B. Your rebel soldiers — what is tracked *in addition* (actor + squad + orders)
**Squad & orders:** `a.squad` (0 Vanguard, 1 Center, 2 Rearguard, 3 Medical) · `squadSlot` · `a.order` (type, target, phase, queue `orderQ`) · `stanceOv` (hold/ambush/scout/harass…) · `pendingOrder` + `retryT` (refused order, trying again) · `a.macro` state (action recorder: step, repeat, start point, error) · volunteers flag in `g.cmd.vol` · trust per soldier (via `p.trueStance`, `conv`, and the promise ledger).
**Stance toward leader:** `trueStance` (loyalty), `conv`, **creed alignment** (`C.alignment(p)`), `zeal`, creed gap (global), promises owed (`g.promises`).
**Combat state:** `weapon{k, mag, spare}` · `weaponOut` · `cd` (fire cooldown) · `reload` · `aim` · `shooting` · `muzzle` · `suppressed` · `exposed` (seconds visible after firing) · `lastHit` · `pain` · `panic` · `hurtT` · `prone` · `hide` · `camo` · `susp` · `target` · `brain{mode, target, seen, lastX/Y, alertT}` · `covertNow` · `surrendered`.
**Movement & body in the world:** `x, y, vx, vy, face, hd` (heading) · `moving` · `running` · `speed` · `loadK` (load slow-down factor) · `path`/`pi` · `working` · `sleeping` · `inside` · `bld` · `inVeh` / `inTrunk` · `collapsed` (heatstroke until) · `onWatch` · `eating`.
**Derived modifiers (NEW, not stored):** `C.accMod` (aim), `C.reloadMod`, `C.speedMod`, `C.alertMod` (sentry), obedience (`C.obedience`) = 0.45 + 0.30·loyalty + 0.18·morale + 0.10·experience + 0.10·discipline + creed alignment − hunger − exhaustion − fear − stress − thirst.
**Status words (NEW):** `a.sbub{text, key, t}` (the small word bubble) built from `C.statesOf(a)`: hungry, thirsty, tired, sleepy, overheated, freezing, soaked, sore feet, filthy, happy, miserable, angry, afraid, stressed, bored, grieving, shaken, hopeless, hopeful, in pain, bleeding, sick, doubting.
**Needs that arise from the camp (not per-person):** latrine use, foul ground, hammocks, firewood, smoke exposure, watch cover, lessons (see `camp.js`).

## C. Civilians and soldiers of the other side (same person record, plus)
`a.aw{state, react, seen, heard, wary, cool}` · `a.react{id, until, subj}` · `a.task{kind, b, work, hungry}` · `a.nextPlan` · `a.fleeing` · `a.mount` · `a.panic` · `a.wait`. Army: `brain.mode` (patrol, investigate, engage, search, retreat), `surrendered`, ranks, helmets/kepis.

## D. Vehicles (`v`)
`type` · `x, y, ang, spd` · `fuel` · `hp` · `engine` (hull-related power) · `tires[4]` · `wreck` · `burning` · `driver` · `pass[]` · `trunk[]` · `cargo{item→n}` · `lights` · `hood` · `order{x,y}` · `route/ri` · `owner` · `army` · `paint` · `taxi` · **mechanical state `c`:** `temp` · `coolant` · `wear` · `stall` · `gaskT` · **faults[]:** hose, belt, plugs, battery, clog, brakes, spring, gasket, stuck (each `known`/`unknown`, may relapse after an improvised fix).

## E. The scene and the world
**Scene:** `alert` (0–1.5) · `foul` (camp ground contamination) · `things[]` (stakes, plots, crops, hammocks, latrines, racks, woodpiles, fires, postas, boards, posters, snares, benches, pots…; each with its own data: `lit`, `use`, `fill`, `serv`, `catch`…) · `estate` · `nightCover` · buildings (`lockUntil`, `safehouse`) · animals (`k`, `hp`, `hpMax`, `fat`, `flee`, `charging`, `dead`, `claimed`).
**Game state (`g`):** `day` · `hour` · `weekday` · `speed` · `weather{rain, fog, target}` · **`clim{temp, feels, humid, wind, front, heatwave}`** (NEW) · `front` / `heatwave` · `res{food, money, ammo, guns, shell, rocket, med, fuel, grenades, dynamite, pamphlets, vales, cloth, scrap, lena, tools, parts…}` · `policy{forage}` · `platform{pub, real, declared}` · `deeds[8]` · `promises[]` · `trust{8 factions}` · `rep{fear, justice, honesty, discipline, competence}` · `regime{legit, armyMorale, usSupport, heat, offensive}` · `creed{6 values}` · `creedGap` · `mercy` · `pol{amnesty}` · `land{ceiling, comp, estates, families, coops}` · `prisoners[]` · `pipe{balance, couriers}` · `base{built, level}` · `stats{killedArmy, killedCiv, lostRebels, released, executed, hired, pesos}` · `strikePrep` · `flags{}` · `unlocked{layer}`.

## F. Metrics we probably still want (candidates)
Per person: **nutrition quality** (hot vs cold meals, protein), **hydration history**, **morale causes** (a short log), **sleep quality** (ground vs hammock), **skills** (marksmanship, first aid, driving, tracking, literacy level, carpentry, cooking — separate from `exp`), **fatigue per limb / carrying stress**, **disease exposure**, **addictions** (tobacco, coffee, rum), **letters/home contact**, **religious practice**, **ranks and command of others**, **debts and promises made *to* them**, **personal grudges** (named), **hunting skill**, **weapon familiarity per weapon**, **ammo discipline**. Per squad: **cohesion**, **fieldcraft**, **noise discipline**, **supply days**. Per camp: **comfort**, **cleanliness**, **security**, **visibility** (smoke, noise, light).
