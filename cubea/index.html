CUBEA

Cuba, 1956–1959. A revolution made of cubes: every cube has a family, a hunger, a memory and a loyalty.
You lead a handful of survivors against a dictator backed by American money. Win the island by force and by trust: feed the hungry, keep your promises, and look after the families of the living and the dead. Or run it as a band of killers for hire. Both are possible; only one is likely to last. Red cubes are for the rebels, blue for Batista, white for the undecided. Pale means a shallow loyalty. Cracks heal; missing chunks don't.
