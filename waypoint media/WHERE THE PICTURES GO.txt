WAYPOINT MEDIA
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This folder holds the photographs, video and watercolours the waypoint cards
show. One folder per place, and inside each:

    pics/          photographs
    vids/          video
    watercolor/    the paintings

The folders are named for PLACES — "don", "e don", "Beltline", "etobicoke
creek" — and the pages are named for ROUTES, and the two do not always agree:
three pages draw on the Don's folder and two on Etobicoke Creek's. Which page
uses which folder is written down in build/media_picks.py, beside the list of
which picture each waypoint shows.

The files themselves are not in the build. They are far too large to carry
around with the code, they are already on the machine that makes the site, and
nothing here needs to read them — the pages only need their NAMES. So this
folder ships nearly empty and fills up on the machine that has the pictures.

File names keep their spaces and their brackets. "don (94).jpg" is what the
scanner called it, and the page encodes the name on its way into the html.
