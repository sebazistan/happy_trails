The player bench

Everybody in artwork/players/, walking on the spot. Nothing here is measured in seconds: drag the speed and every figure re-times itself, because each one is driven by how far it has gone and not by a clock.

The four names that are the whole interface. part-<name> is a thing that can move; pivot-<name> is a dot saying where it turns, never drawn; ground is the line the feet stand on; anchor is the single point that rides the route. Nesting is the skeleton — a shin drawn inside a thigh follows the thigh.

How each one moves is in build/players_data.py. How far apart they live on the trail is in the same file, under CAST.
