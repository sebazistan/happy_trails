TRAIL LINK IMAGES

One picture per trail: the tile on the Trails page, and the picture behind
the opening card of that trail's own page. 1000 × 560, named for the trail's
slug — beltline.jpg, lower-don.jpg — plus soon.jpg, which is the tile for a
trail that has no page yet.

THESE ARE FILES, NOT OUTPUT. Replace one and it is replaced; the build copies
this folder into the site as it stands and does not draw over anything it
finds here. That is the whole point of the folder existing separately from
`waypoint media/`: these are chosen and cropped deliberately, one per trail,
and they are not part of the pool the waypoint cards draw from.

A trail with NO file here still gets a drawing — a generated view of a ravine
or a hydro corridor, seeded off the trail so each is its own. That is what a
new trail wants on the day it is added and before anybody has painted it. Put
a file here and the drawing stops being made.

The ones here now were picked at random from the watercolours and cropped to
the tile's shape. They are a starting point.
