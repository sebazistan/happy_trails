The network, trail by trail

Trails

Around 400 kilometres of multi-use trail run through the Greater Toronto Area — paved, separated from traffic, and open to anyone on foot or on wheels. Most of them sit in ravines and hydro corridors: the land nobody could build on. Here is what there is, and where it stops.

Railpaths

Railpath · 10.2 km7.4 m/km

Beltline Trail

A mixed-use trail following the path of the former Toronto Belt Line Railway, which operated for about seventy years. The Spadina Expressway carved through the area in the late 1960s and the trail still carries the scar: it is cut in two at Allan Rd, fifty years on. The eastern half, through Mt. Pleasant Cemetery and down Mud Creek to the Brickworks, is the best of it.
