What is missing, and what it would take

Wishful thinking

45 places where the trail network stops, doubles back, or sends you onto a road. Each one is somewhere a specific, buildable thing would fix a specific problem — a crossing, a deck, a light, a kilometre of path. Narrow them down by what they would cost and by how badly they are wanted, and the argument makes itself. The cheapest thing that would do the most good is always first.
